Two ways to draw the lineage

Nick asked to see two relationship diagrams before either gets built into the wiki proper: an organization lineage web (who merged into whom) and an affiliation overlap showing how the same three men moved through several of these organizations at once. Both are drawn here from data already sitting in organizations.json and historical_actors.json — nothing invented for the sketch. A third section tries the flip-card interaction on one real entity, text on one face and diagram on the other.

Sketch A

The lineage web

Betar trained the men who commanded Irgun and Lehi; Irgun became Herut's political vehicle; Herut and its allies became Likud. This is the clearest "ready to draw" relationship in the dataset — it's already sitting in each org's own predecessor_orgs / successor_orgs / merged_into fields.

Youth movement Militia Political party
Viability: straightforward. Five orgs, five real edges, no invented connections. Scales awkwardly past ~8–10 nodes though — the full org file has 42 entries across several unrelated lineages (Labor's Poalei Zion→Mapai→Israeli Labor Party chain, the Jewish Home/National Union/Yamina tangle). A single page probably wants one lineage at a time, picked by whichever org page you arrived from, rather than all 42 crammed onto one canvas.
Sketch B

The affiliation overlap

Three men, three life stages, one movement. Jabotinsky founded the youth wing and briefly commanded a militia; Begin and Shamir both passed through a youth-movement stage, a militia stage, and a party stage — just not the same militia. A true five-circle Venn (Betar/Irgun/Lehi/Herut/Likud) draws as an unreadable knot, so this groups by stage instead — three circles, each labelled with which specific org applied to whom.

Viability: works for three people, gets crowded past four or five — label collision inside the shared centre region is the real limit, not the geometry. Reads best as a focused comparison ("these three founders" or "everyone who passed through Betar") rather than a whole-dataset view. Might work well seeded from a search or a hand-picked "compare" list rather than always showing everyone.
Sketch C

The flip card

Same Irgun entry, two faces of one card — the article prose on the front, a small diagram centred on Irgun's own lineage on the back. A button flips it; the transform is 3D so it reads as one object turning over, not a page swap.

Militia · Paramilitary

Irgun Tzvai Leumi (Etzel)

Emerged in spring 1931 when Avraham Tehomi and nineteen other officers resigned from the Haganah — over its policy of restraint — to form a rival militia. Not Revisionist-aligned at founding: it formally became the Revisionist movement's own militia only in 1936–37, when Ze'ev Jabotinsky assumed its command.

Irgun in the lineage

Same underlying record — lineage.predecessor_orgs / merged_into — drawn instead of read.

Viability: the interaction itself is easy — a CSS 3D transform, one button, and it degrades gracefully (instant swap, no spin) for anyone with reduced-motion set. The real cost is on the diagram side: every article would need a second, hand-fitted diagram, which is a lot of drawing across 42 orgs and 60 people. Realistic path is probably: build it for org pages first (lineage is nearly mechanical to generate from the existing JSON), and treat person pages as a later, hand-curated pass rather than promising it everywhere at once.
