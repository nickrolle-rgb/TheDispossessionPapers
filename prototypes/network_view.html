Design prototype · 2026-09-03

The whole archive, as a network

Same homepage, same entities, two ways to move through them. Flip to see every Actor and Organization — historical or Knesset, domestic or foreign patron — as one navigable graph — click anything to pull its full record into the panel on the right, or search for it directly. Laws and Topics stay out of the way until you zoom into whoever sponsored or discussed them (toggle the eye icon to show them always). Real data throughout: every edge is derived from structured fields already in the dataset (org membership, org lineage, law sponsorship, topic cross-references) — nothing hand-placed.
